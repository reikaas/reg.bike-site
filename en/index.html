reg.bike is a new Norwegian bike registry. Register the frame number, put a QR sticker on your bike, and let anyone who finds it or is thinking of buying it check in seconds whether it has been reported stolen.

How it works

Three steps from frame number to marked bike

Registering a bike takes a couple of minutes. The sticker does the rest.

  1. 01

    Register

    Create an account and add the make, model, colour, frame number and photos of your bike.

  2. 02

    Mark your bike

    You get a unique QR sticker. Print it yourself, or order durable stickers by post, and put it on the frame.

  3. 03

    Scan and check

    Anyone can scan the sticker or look up the code and see right away whether the bike is registered or reported stolen.

Benefits

Useful for everyone who deals with bikes

One sticker on the frame helps the owner, whoever finds the bike, and whoever wants to buy it.

If you own the bike

  • Frame number, photos and details kept in one place
  • Report it stolen, and everyone who scans it sees that right away
  • The sticker shows the bike is registered and traceable
  • Everything ready when you report the theft to the police

If you find a bike

  • Scan the sticker with your phone camera – no app needed
  • See whether the bike has been reported stolen
  • Message the owner through reg.bike without seeing who they are

If you're buying used

  • Check the code or frame number before you pay
  • See whether the bike has been reported stolen
  • Safer deals for buyers and honest sellers alike

The sticker

A mark thieves notice

Every bike gets its own code. The sticker comes in a portrait format for the seat tube and a landscape format.

  • A unique code for every bikeThe QR code opens reg.bike/id/<code>. The code is printed in plain text too, so it can be typed in.
  • Made to live on a bikeOrder durable stickers by post, or print them at home for free.
  • Works with any phone cameraNo app to download. Whoever scans it sees the status right in the browser:

Privacy

The owner stays anonymous

Public pages never show the owner's name, email, phone number or address.

  • Whoever scans the sticker only sees the bike's status and description.
  • Messages from someone who finds your bike are passed on to you without revealing your contact details.
  • You choose whether photos of your bike are shown publicly.
  • This website uses no cookies, tracking or analytics.
